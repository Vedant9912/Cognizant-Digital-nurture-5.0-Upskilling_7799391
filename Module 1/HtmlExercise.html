Community Bulletin

The city council is pleased to announce a series of exciting community events planned for the upcoming season. These events are designed to bring residents together, celebrate local culture, and promote health and wellbeing across all age groups. From music festivals and food fairs to eco-drives and sports days, there is something for everyone in our vibrant community.

Residents are encouraged to register early as seats are limited. Volunteers are also welcome to participate in the organization of these events. Please contact the city council office for more information or visit this portal regularly for the latest updates. Together, we can make our community a better place for all.
